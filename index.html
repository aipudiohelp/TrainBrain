About the Founder

Ibrahim Hammouda

Ibrahim Hammouda is an international communications strategist and founder of TrainBrain for Consultation and Skills Development. He specializes in high-stakes corporate communication, executive storytelling, and customized workforce capability programs designed for complex regional markets.

Throughout his career, Ibrahim has served as a strategic link between international institutions, governance bodies, and corporate leaders—advising on trade infrastructure, cross-cultural diplomacy, and strategic positioning. His background combines policy insight from Washington D.C. and regional economic diplomacy with deep pedagogical expertise in professional communication.

Through TrainBrain, Ibrahim partners with enterprise leaders to deliver high-fidelity executive stress-testing workshops, corporate communication strategies, and targeted skill development that empower teams to translate complex business objectives into clear, high-impact results. One of the main pillars of inspiration behind this work is Mr. Shafik Gabr who has been a mentor, supporter and a role-model.
